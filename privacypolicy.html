Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Abraxas processes information when you use its Discord-native forms, applications, review workflows, and related integrations.

Effective September 12, 2026
Abraxas is designed to collect and retain only the information reasonably needed to operate the features a Discord community and its members choose to use.

1. Information Abraxas Processes

Discord identity and server context

Abraxas uses Discord as its identity and community-context provider. Depending on the feature, Abraxas may process information such as:

  • Discord user, server, role, channel, and related identifiers;
  • Discord usernames, display names, server names, or role names when needed for presentation;
  • current server membership, roles, and permissions needed to determine access or perform a requested Discord-side action; and
  • installation and authorization information needed to connect Abraxas with Discord.

Forms, applications, and submissions

When people create, complete, or review forms through Abraxas, the service may process:

  • form names, instructions, questions, choices, eligibility rules, and other author-created configuration;
  • text responses, selections, acknowledgements, requested roles, and similar submitted information;
  • draft progress and the page or route taken through a form where needed to continue or validate a submission;
  • submission, review, approval, denial, and related workflow state;
  • review reasons or notes where a feature permits them; and
  • timestamps and identifiers needed to associate this information with the applicable Discord user, server, form, or workflow.

Form authors control what ordinary form questions ask, subject to the Terms of Service and product limits.

Operational information

Abraxas may process limited logs, metrics, correlation identifiers, request information, error details, and similar operational data needed to secure, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the reliability of the service.

Abraxas aims to minimize the amount of user content and Discord data included in operational telemetry.

2. How Abraxas Uses Information

Abraxas uses information to provide and operate the service, including to:

  • authenticate users through Discord and establish server context;
  • determine access, eligibility, reviewer authority, and administrative capabilities;
  • create, publish, complete, review, and manage forms and applications;
  • perform Discord-side actions explicitly associated with a configured workflow, such as assigning a role;
  • deliver service notifications and maintain workflow state;
  • prevent abuse, investigate failures, and protect the service and its users;
  • respond to privacy requests; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

Abraxas does not sell Discord API data, use it to build advertising profiles, or disclose it to data brokers or advertising networks.

Abraxas does not use Discord message content or form submissions to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models.

3. Information Shared With Others

Discord

Abraxas communicates with Discord for authentication, server context, authorized Discord reads, notifications, and configured Discord-side effects.

Application and form review occurs inside Abraxas. Abraxas is not designed to send full submission answers into Discord channels merely so they can be reviewed there.

Information that Abraxas does send to Discord becomes subject to Discord's own terms, privacy practices, and server permission system.

Authorized server administrators and reviewers

Information submitted through a form may be made available inside Abraxas to the administrators or delegated reviewers authorized for that form or server.

Infrastructure providers

Abraxas currently uses third-party infrastructure providers, including OVHcloud for hosting and Cloudflare for network connectivity and security services. Information may pass through or reside on infrastructure operated by those providers as necessary to run the service.

Legal and safety requirements

Information may be preserved or disclosed where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, valid legal process, enforce service rules, investigate abuse, or protect the rights, safety, and security of Abraxas, its users, or others.

4. Data Retention

Different categories of information are retained for different periods depending on the feature and operational need. Abraxas generally retains information only while it is reasonably needed to operate a feature, maintain workflow integrity, secure the service, resolve failures, comply with legal obligations, or maintain appropriate backups.

Form and submission data

Drafts, published form content, submissions, review state, decisions, and related workflow information may be retained while they are needed to provide the applicable form or application workflow.

Abraxas may retain limited state after detailed submission content is no longer needed where that state is necessary to show a current outcome, prevent duplicate actions, enforce reapplication or eligibility rules, investigate abuse, or maintain service integrity.

Server configuration

Server-owned configuration may be retained while Abraxas remains installed or while the configuration is otherwise needed to operate, unwind, secure, or account for the applicable feature.

Operational data and backups

Logs, queues, caches, metrics, backups, and other operational records may have separate retention periods based on their purpose. Abraxas aims to keep these records limited and to remove them when they are no longer reasonably needed.

Deletion from active product state may not immediately remove information from transient caches, backups, or independent copies held by Discord or another third party.

5. Data the Service Is Not Designed to Handle

Abraxas is intended for Discord community forms and applications, not identity verification, employment screening, financial services, health services, background checks, or other high-stakes or heavily regulated data collection.

The service is not designed to collect passwords, authentication credentials, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, private cryptographic keys, access tokens, protected health or medical information, or similarly sensitive information.

Abraxas does not provide general-purpose file uploads or file hosting. A form author may choose to ask for a URL as ordinary text, but Abraxas does not undertake to fetch, preview, scan, or verify user-supplied external content.

6. Security

Abraxas uses technical and administrative safeguards intended to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Persisted Abraxas service data is stored on encrypted storage, and network communications use encrypted transport where supported by the applicable protocol and service path. Abraxas also uses access controls, data minimization, and operational safeguards appropriate to the service.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Security measures may change as the service and its infrastructure develop.

7. Privacy Rights and Requests

You may contact Abraxas to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that Abraxas holds about you. Additional rights may apply depending on where you live.

Privacy contact

privacy@abraxas.bot

Abraxas may need to verify that you control the Discord account associated with a request before disclosing, modifying, or deleting account-linked information.

Requests are handled in accordance with applicable law and may be subject to legal, security, operational, or record-retention exceptions.

Deleting information held by Abraxas does not itself delete independent information already stored by Discord or another third party.

8. Information About Children

Abraxas is not directed to people who are below the minimum age permitted to use Discord in their jurisdiction.

If you believe Abraxas has received information relating to someone who is not permitted to use Discord because of their age, contact privacy@abraxas.bot.

9. International Processing

Discord and infrastructure used to operate Abraxas may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable law requires particular safeguards for international transfers, Abraxas will use or rely on appropriate mechanisms as required.

10. Changes and Contact

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Abraxas, its infrastructure, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.

Material changes to the service's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy.
